Carly received her PhD from the University of Illinois in Behavioral Neuroscience. As a postdoc in the Fox Lab, she was interested in exploring why anxiety disorders often onset during adolescence and how estrogens influence anxiety-like behaviors. After completing her post-doctoral training in the Fox Lab, she accepted a position as a Data Scientist at Clarity Innovations.
