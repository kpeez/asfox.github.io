Dr. Drwecki is an associate professor of psychology at [Regis University](https://www.regis.edu/RC/Academics/Departments-and-Faculty/Psychology.aspx) in Denver, Colorado. He spent his sabbatical year collaborating with Dr. Fox, examining the neural-economic underpinnings of generosity and compassion with a specific focus on how to increase equity and reduce inequality. Dr. Drwecki has returned to his position at Regis University.
