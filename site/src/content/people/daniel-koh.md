Daniel Koh was a Junior Specialist who worked on implementing and deploying state-of-the-art (SOTA) machine learning models and pipelines to autonomously analyze nonhuman primate behavior at the California National Primate Research Center. Daniel was in charge of data collection and furthering the development of SOTA machine learning tools.
