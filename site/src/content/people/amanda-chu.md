Amanda graduated in Neurobiology, Physiology, and Behavior with a minor in Psychology. She worked with Dr. Fox to identify and classify neuronal cell types that are involved in fear and anxiety in mice through single-cell RNA sequencing techniques. She was interested in understanding the molecular basis of complex emotions, such as fear, in hopes that it would contribute to the development of more effective treatments for people suffering from anxiety disorders. Amanda left to continue her scientific career as a Psychology graduate student working with Dr. McDannald at Boston College.
