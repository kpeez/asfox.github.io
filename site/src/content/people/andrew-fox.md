Drew Fox is an Associate Professor in the Psychology Department at [UC Davis](http://psychology.ucdavis.edu/) and a Core Scientist in Neuroscience and Behavior and the [National Biomedical Research Institute](https://nbri.ucdavis.edu). The Fox lab is interested in understanding the neuroscience of social and emotional behavior in both humans and nonhuman primates. Fox researches modern neuroscientific approaches and computational modeling to understand emotion-related decision-making and the development of social anxiety disorder.
