Mesude received her undergraduate degree in Psychology from Uludag University in Turkey and completed her master’s degree in Cognitive Neuroscience at the University of Trento in Italy. She joined the Fox Lab in 2024 and is co-advised by Dr. Erie Boorman. Her research interests focus on the neurobiology and computational mechanisms underlying fear and anxiety.
