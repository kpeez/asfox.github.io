Lillian Campos graduated from the University of California, Davis, where she received a bachelor’s in Psychology. She worked as a research assistant in the Fox lab, and then became a graduate student leading a study that used neuroimaging techniques (e.g. fMRI, DTI, PET) to understand how the brain processes emotion. Lillian defended her PhD.
