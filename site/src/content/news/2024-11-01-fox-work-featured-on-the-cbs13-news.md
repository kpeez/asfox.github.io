Work from the Fox Lab was featured on the nightly news. This brief news feature focuses on our recent [paper](https://asfox.github.io/publications/KambojFox_AJP_2024.pdf) published in the American Journal of Psychiatry.

As a scientist, I often get caught up on small inaccuracies and the details, but I think they got the gist of what we aimed to do, and I hope that this feature increases awareness of the work that we and other scientists are doing help those that are suffering from debilitating mental illness.

See below for their feature: “UC Davis study aims to find link between how your cells contribute to anxiety, depression.”
