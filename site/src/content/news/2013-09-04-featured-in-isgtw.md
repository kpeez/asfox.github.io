Some of my research was recently featured in international science grid this week (iSGTW), an international weekly online publication that covers distributed computing and the research it enables. In this article, they summarize some of the ideas that have motivated our lab's efforts to understand the heritability of brain function in relation to individual differences in anxiety, and how we have accomplished this with the help of the open science grid ([OSG](http://www.opensciencegrid.org/)).

[Click for the full article](http://www.isgtw.org/feature/brain-circuitry-and-mechanisms-underlying-anxiety-uncovered)

Update: This research was just published in PNAS, see [publications](/publications/)

By using the tools provided by the OSG, we were able to use 231 years of computer time in under a month. As computing tools improve, our ability to ask questions that were previously untenable will only increase. This is an exciting time for science. :-D
