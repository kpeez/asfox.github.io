This project (Aligning Science Across Parkinson's, ASAP-020495; Team Leader: Gradinaru, Co-PI: Fox) is titled "Gut-to-brain circuit contributions to Parkinson-like phenotypes in non-transgenic rodent and primate animal models." The lab aims to leverage recently developed neuroscientific tools to understand brain circuits relevant to Parkinson's.

The work also involves developing new tools for high-throughput analysis of primate brain circuits.
