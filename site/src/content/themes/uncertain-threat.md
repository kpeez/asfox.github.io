Two NIH-funded projects study the neural correlates of anticipating a threat whose timing is uncertain. One (R21 MH129851, PI: Fox) uses a novel computational model and functional MRI, and is titled "Rethinking the Neural Correlates of Uncertain Threat Anticipation with a Statistical Learning Approach."

The other (R01 MH131264, PI: Shackman) combines a computational approach with functional MRI and cell-phone based experience sampling. It is titled "Using Theory- and Data-Driven Neurocomputational Approaches and Digital Phenotyping to Understand RDoC Acute and Potential Threat."
