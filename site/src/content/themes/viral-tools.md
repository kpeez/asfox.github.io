Through an NIH-funded project (U01 MH128336, Co-PIs: Gradinaru, Tian, Fox), the lab aims to develop and validate novel viral vectors that can be used in nonhuman primates to deliver cutting-edge reporters, effectors, and sensors. The project is titled "Engineered AAV Identification, Validation, and Dissemination Pipeline for Brain Cell Type-Specific Manipulation Across Species."

According to the funding description, this tool development is necessary to determine the specific cells and projections that give rise to the experience of anxiety.
