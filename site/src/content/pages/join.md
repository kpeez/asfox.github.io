The Fox lab at the University of California at Davis is often looking for undergraduate, graduate, and post-doctoral team members.

In the Fox Lab we primarily study nonhuman primates using brain imaging and molecular techniques.

For an idea of what the Fox lab is working on, check out my [publications](/publications/), and the Fox Lab [mission statement](/mission/).

If you are collaborative, innovative and interested in exploring new frontiers, there may be a place for you in the Fox lab.

Email [Drew](mailto:fox.drew@gmail.com) to start a conversation about how you might fit in.

P.S. In the fox lab, we are particularly focused on computationally intensive approaches– **if you have programming skills, be sure to let me know!** :-)
